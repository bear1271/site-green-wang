MODULE 01 / ECON 141

Probability Distributions

Outcomes, PMF, CDF, density, and quantiles

ON THIS PAGE
  1. 1. Outcomes, events, and random variables
  2. 2. PMF, CDF, and the Bernoulli distribution
  3. 3. Continuous distributions and density
  4. 4. Quantiles and distribution reading

Probability describes a population before data are observed. Statistics uses a random sample to learn about that population. Keeping these directions separate prevents many later confusions.

1. Outcomes, events, and random variables

An outcome is a possible result of a random process; the sample space \(\Omega\) contains all outcomes. An event A is a subset of \(\Omega . A\) random variable \(Y\) maps an outcome to a numerical value. Probabilities are nonnegative\(, \Pr(\Omega )=1\), and probabilities of mutually exclusive events add.

\[P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B),\qquad P(A^c)=1-P(A).\]

A discrete variable takes a finite or countable set of values. A continuous variable with a density takes values on a continuum. Uppercase \(Y\) denotes the random variable; lowercase \(y\) denotes a possible or realized value. A parameter such as \(\mu\) describes its population distribution.

中文直觉：事件是“会不会发生的事”，随机变量是把结果变成可以运算的数字。例如“通勤超过 20 分钟”是事件，通勤分钟数是随机变量。概率不是每次都必须兑现的比例，而是对重复过程的描述。

2. PMF, CDF, and the Bernoulli distribution

The probability mass function (PMF) is \(p_{Y}(y)=\Pr(Y=y)\). For support \({y_{1},\ldots ,y_{k}}\), each probability is nonnegative and their sum is one. Event probabilities are sums over the relevant support points.

\[F_Y(c)=P(Y\le c)=\sum_{y\le c}p_Y(y).\]

The cumulative distribution function (CDF) is nondecreasing and right-continuous, with limits zero and one at the two extremes. For any \(a<b, \Pr(a<Y\le b)=F_{Y}(b)-F_{Y}(a)\). At a discrete mass point\(, \Pr(Y=a)=F_{Y}(a)-F_{Y}(a-)\); endpoint inclusion matters.

Thus \(\Pr(1\le M\le 2)=.10+.06=.16\), whereas \(\Pr(1<M\le 2)=.06\). The CDF is a staircase, with jump sizes equal to the PMF.

A Bernoulli variable equals 1 with probability \(p\) and 0 with probability \(1-p\). It encodes any binary outcome: employment, voting, treatment assignment, or a short commute. Its parameter is a probability, not an observed sample fraction.

3. Continuous distributions and density

\[\begin{aligned} f_Y(y)&\ge0, & \int_{-\infty}^{\infty}f_Y(y)\,dy&=1,\\ F_Y(c)&=\int_{-\infty}^{c}f_Y(y)\,dy,\\ P(a<Y\le b)&=\int_a^b f_Y(y)\,dy.\end{aligned}\]

For a variable with a density\(, \Pr(Y=c)=0\). The density is a height, not a point probability, and can exceed one. For example, the uniform density on [0,.5] has height 2; the total area is still one. Where F is differentiable\(, f=F'\).

中文直觉：CDF 像累计里程表，记录“到这里为止积累了多少概率”；PDF 像每单位横轴上的概率浓度。连续型随机变量要问一个区间内的面积，不能把曲线高度当作概率。

Worked example. If the commuting-time CDF satisfies \(F(15)=.20\) and \(F(20)=.78\), then \(\Pr(15<Y\le 20)=.58\) and \(\Pr(Y>20)=.22\). Both a CDF difference and a density area describe the same event.

4. Quantiles and distribution reading

The \(\tau\)-quantile is \(q_\tau=\inf\{y:F(y)\ge\tau\}\). The median is \(q_{.5}\); the interquartile range is \(q_{.75}-q_{.25}\). For continuous strictly increasing CDFs, \(F(q_\tau)=\tau\). The generalized definition also works for discrete variables.

Percentiles become critical values in inference: the 97.5th percentile of a standard normal distribution leaves 2.5% in its upper tail. A median describes a distribution'\(s\) location but need not equal its mean.

Check your understanding

Question. A density equals 3 on an interval of width .1. What is the probability of that interval? Can a discrete PMF equal 3?

Solution. The probability is .3. A PMF cannot exceed 1, because each value is an event probability.
