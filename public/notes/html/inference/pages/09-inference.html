Tests & Confidence Intervals

p-values, size, power, coverage, and precision

ON THIS PAGE

Inference measures how strongly a sample distinguishes a hypothesized population mean from alternatives. A hypothesis test, a p-value, and a confidence interval are related views of the same sampling uncertainty.

1. State the target and the hypotheses

For a population mean \(\mu , a\) two-sided test compares \(H_{0}\):\(\mu =\mu _{0}\) with \(H_{1}\):\(\mu \ne \mu _{0}\). Upper- and lower-sided alternatives are \(\mu >\mu _{0}\) and \(\mu <\mu _{0}\). Choose the direction before looking at the result. A test either rejects \(H_{0}\) or fails to reject it; failure to reject does not establish equality.

\[\begin{aligned}\text{Known }\sigma:\quad Z&=\frac{\bar Y-\mu_0}{\sigma/\sqrt n},\\ \text{Unknown }\sigma:\quad t&=\frac{\bar Y-\mu_0}{\operatorname{SE}(\bar Y)},\\ \operatorname{SE}(\bar Y)&=\frac s{\sqrt n}.\end{aligned}\]

Under the true null, the large-sample reference distribution is \(\mathcal{N}(0,1)\), using the CLT and, when necessary, Slutsky. With normal observations and known \(\sigma\), the \(Z\) distribution is exact for any \(n\). The exact small-sample unknown\(-\sigma\) case is in Module 11.

2. What a p-value means

A p-value is a null-model probability of a statistic at least as adverse to \(H_{0}\) as the observed statistic, using the preselected direction of the test.

AlternativeApproximate p-value5% rejection rule
\(\mu \ne \mu _{0}\)\(2[1-\Phi (|t_{\text{obs}}|)]\)\(|t_{\text{obs}}|>1.96\)
\(\mu >\mu _{0}\)\(1-\Phi (t_{\text{obs}})\)\(t_{\text{obs}}>1.645\)
\(\mu <\mu _{0}\)\(\Phi (t_{\text{obs}})\)\(t_{\text{obs}}<-1.645\)

For a two-sided test, distance is measured in both tails. For an upper-sided test, a large negative statistic gives a large p-value. A p-value is not \(\Pr(H_{0}\) is true|data), the probability that chance “caused” the result, or the probability of the exact observed sample.

中文直觉：先暂时把原假设当真，再问“如果按这套模型重复抽样，会有多经常出现这么极端或更极端的统计量？”这是一道条件概率问题，不是在给原假设本身分配真假概率。

3. Significance, size, power, and errors

Truth / decisionReject \(H_{0}\)Fail to reject \(H_{0}\)
\(H_{0}\) trueType I errorCorrect nonrejection
\(H_{0}\) falseCorrect rejectionType II error

The significance level \(\alpha\) is the chosen Type I error target. The size is the actual null rejection probability (or its supremum over a composite null). Approximation errors can make actual size differ from nominal \(\alpha\). The power at an alternative is the probability of rejection there; it equals one minus its Type II error probability. Critical values define a rejection region.

Lowering \(\alpha\) while retaining the same test family raises the cutoff and reduces power. Larger \(n\) typically raises power against a fixed nonzero effect by reducing standard error. Statistical significance does not measure economic importance: a tiny effect can be precisely estimated, and an important effect can be imprecisely estimated.

\[\begin{aligned}\text{Reject if }\bar Y&>\mu_0+z_{1-\alpha}\frac\sigma{\sqrt n},\\ \operatorname{Power}(\mu_1)&=1-\Phi\!\left[z_{1-\alpha}-\frac{(\mu_1-\mu_0)\sqrt n}{\sigma}\right].\end{aligned}\]

This formula is exact for normal observations and approximate under a suitable CLT. It displays the roles of the effect, noise, sample size, and cutoff. With many true nulls, repeated testing can create false positives; for m independent tests at \(\alpha\), the probability of at least one false rejection is \(1-(1-\alpha )\)ᵐ.

4. Worked inference from start to finish

Use the textbook'\(s\) hypothetical wage sample: \(n=200, \bar{Y} =22.64, s=18.14\), and \(H_{0}\):\(\mu =20\) versus a two-sided alternative. Then \(\operatorname{SE}=18.14/\sqrt{200}\approx 1.2827, t\approx 2.0582\), and \(p\approx .0396\). Reject at 5%, but not at 1%. The estimated difference is 2.64 dollars per hour; report it with uncertainty, not just a rejection label.

5. Confidence intervals by test inversion

Collect all null values \(\mu _{0}\) that the two-sided test does not reject:

\[\begin{gathered}\left|\frac{\bar Y-\mu_0}{\operatorname{SE}}\right|\le z_{1-\alpha/2}\\ \Longleftrightarrow\quad \bar Y-z_{1-\alpha/2}\operatorname{SE}\le\mu_0\le\bar Y+z_{1-\alpha/2}\operatorname{SE},\\ \operatorname{CI}_{1-\alpha}=\left[\bar Y-z_{1-\alpha/2}\operatorname{SE},\;\bar Y+z_{1-\alpha/2}\operatorname{SE}\right].\end{gathered}\]

The 95% wage interval is approximately [20.126,25.154]. Since 20 lies outside it, the matching two-sided 5% test rejects. Test–interval duality requires the same estimator, standard error, reference distribution, and sidedness. Mixing conventions can break the apparent agreement.

Coverage: \(P_\mu\{\mu\in\operatorname{CI}(\text{data})\}\approx1-\alpha\) across repeated samples. The interval endpoints are random; \(\mu\) is fixed in this frequentist formulation. After observing the data, the particular interval either contains \(\mu\) or does not. A 95% interval for the mean is not an interval containing 95% of individual outcomes.

中文直觉：置信度是“造区间的方法”在重复抽样中的命中率。把很多次实验得到的区间摆在一起，约 95% 会覆盖同一个真值。更高置信度需要更宽的网，不意味着这次估计更精确。

6. Precision and sample-size planning

The margin of error is \(z\cdot \operatorname{SE}\). At fixed confidence level and population spread, halving the margin requires four times as many independent observations. For a Bernoulli proportion\(, p(1-p)\le 1/4\) gives a conservative normal-approximation planning formula:

\[n\ge\frac{z_{1-\alpha/2}^2}{4e^2}.\]

where e is the desired half-width, and \(n\) is rounded up. For 95% and \(e=.005\), using \(z=1.96\) gives \(n\ge 38,416\). This controls the planned approximate sampling margin under the design; it does not eliminate selection bias or guarantee realized coverage under every small-sample configuration.

A one-sided 95% lower confidence set is [\(\bar{Y} -1.645SE,\infty\)); an upper confidence set is (\(-\infty ,\bar{Y} +1.645SE\)]. These invert the corresponding one-sided tests.

Check your understanding

Question. A two-sided p-value for \(\mu _{0}\) is .07. Is \(\mu _{0}\) in the matching 90% and 95% intervals?

Solution. It is excluded from the 90% interval, since .07<.10; included in the 95% interval, since .07>.05.
