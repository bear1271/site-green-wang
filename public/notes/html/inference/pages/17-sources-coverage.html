5. Corrections and clarifications preserved in the notes

  • The extended-reading README's “no formal citations” statement is incorrect when applied to the complete Chapter 3: E3.2 explicitly cites List (2003). Chapter 2's stock-return box also mentions Taleb's book. The original README was left unchanged; this guide records the correction.
  • Equation (3.23), printed p. 135, uses the first group's mean inside the second group's squared deviations in the supplied PDF. The pooled variance must use each group's own mean. This was checked visually against the PDF.
  • Equal group sizes equate pooled and unpooled SE formulas but do not, by themselves, establish an exact Student t distribution.
  • The exact n−1 sample-variance convention for a Bernoulli sample differs slightly from the usual n-divisor plug-in proportion SE. Both are stated explicitly.
  • SD(a+bY)=|b|SD(Y); the absolute value is needed for negative b. Symmetry implies zero skewness when the moment exists, but the converse is not universal.
  • Finite-variance LLN and finite-fourth-moment sample-variance arguments are sufficient conditions used in the chapter. They are not stated as the weakest possible assumptions.
  • Simple random sampling from a finite population without replacement is not literally i.i.d.; the guide distinguishes the textbook's working model from this design issue.
  • Convergence in probability can be to a random variable in general. The LLN's constant limit is the case used here, not a universal definition.
