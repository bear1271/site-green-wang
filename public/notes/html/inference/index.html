Reading contract · 内容与范围

All main sections and appendices have been mapped to these modules. Repeated explanations and numerical drills are consolidated; distinct reasoning chains remain. Original datasets are not required to understand the text. Where the textbook asks for external data, a complete workflow is supplied and uncomputed results are not invented.

The notes explicitly separate exact results, asymptotic approximations, and extensions. For original-page traceability or corrections to extracted formulas, open Sources & Coverage Map.
