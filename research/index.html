Research

How do institutions shape what people value and believe?

My current questions concern the formation of preferences and beliefs. I hope to use formal models to explore their interaction, starting from historically specified conditions and examining how different paths may unfold. I am also interested in how ways of measuring and comparing value affect both behavior and our interpretation of historical evidence.

Institutions & valuation

Can expressing different forms of value in monetary terms influence preferences?
