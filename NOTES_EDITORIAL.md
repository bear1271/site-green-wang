# Notes publication editing agreement

User direction confirmed on 28 October 2026. This supersedes the earlier condensed-edition approach.

## Purpose and fidelity

Prepare the existing complete notes for public reading. Preserve their scope, structure, arguments, examples, proofs, references, qualifications, and distinctions between source arguments and personal assessments. Do not replace them with summaries, abridgments, newly written essays, or reorganized syntheses without an explicit request.

## Working language

English is the default language of public notes, including notes on Chinese political-economic topics. Translate faithfully rather than compressing or expanding the argument.

Within Poetics and Comparative Literature, materials explicitly concerning Chinese subjects retain Traditional Chinese with appropriate English. Preserve Chinese original names, titles, and passages; do not replace Chinese personal names with romanization alone. Use traditional forms in literary contexts: 王昌齡, 祖詠, 李嶷, 國秀集, 芮挺章, 殷璠. Retain existing personal translations and identify quoted translations separately.

## Permitted publication cleanup

Correct typos and broken encodings; normalize headings, citations, punctuation, notation, and bilingual typography. Replace classroom instructions and internal path references with reader-facing wording while preserving substantive scope. Repair navigation, links, formulas, tables, mobile layout, and non-JavaScript readability. Fix a substantive factual or mathematical problem only with evidence, and record the change. Mark unresolved issues rather than silently altering the claim.

## Versions and publication

Keep local originals unchanged. Maintain a source-to-public change log. Do not treat upload dates as substantive revision dates. A public reading file must be reviewed for faithful content and rendering before it is linked from the website or shared publicly. Directory selection does not approve a newly rewritten substitute.

## Drive cleanup

The 14 archive files previously classified Keep Private are to be removed from active Google Drive storage using Trash. The three unapproved condensed PDFs are withdrawn and removed as well. Proposed-public originals remain. Local originals, local snapshots, and local review records remain available.
